# HR-Recruitment-Dashboard
A Tableau dashboard on hiring efficiency in 2026: funnel, sources and time to hire.

# Goal
Understand which sources and departments produce hires, and where hiring slows down.
# What I built
KPI tiles, a recruitment funnel, source share and source analysis, department hiring, duration by department and filters for stage, role, department and source.
# Key findings
319 candidates led to 27 hires: an 8.46% hire rate and 11.8 candidates per hire.
The company website was the best source, with an 11.94% hire rate and 8 hires.
IT had the most hires (10) and the longest time to final stage.
